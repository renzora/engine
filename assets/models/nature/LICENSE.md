# Nature models

Rocks and ground plants for dressing a terrain, offered as engine presets:
`list_assets` with `source engine` lists them, and passing one to
`spawn_model` copies it into the project. Each is a self-contained `.glb` with
its 1024 px colour, normal and AO/roughness/metallic textures inside.

All are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are **CC0** (public
domain): no attribution is required, and they may be used, changed and shipped
in anything.

| File | Poly Haven asset |
|---|---|
| `boulder_01.glb` | [boulder_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/boulder_01) |
| `rock_moss_set_01.glb` | [rock_moss_set_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01) |
| `fern_02.glb` | [fern_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02) |
| `shrub_sorrel_01.glb` | [shrub_sorrel_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_sorrel_01) |
| `tree_stump_01.glb` | [tree_stump_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_stump_01) |

Trees are not here: every Poly Haven tree is hundreds of megabytes. The
`procedural_tree` preset makes trees instead.
